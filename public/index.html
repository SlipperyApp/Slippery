Three beats, one slip.

You place the bet and forward the slip. Slippery lifts the stake, odds, selection and bookmaker off the image. At full time it grades the bet on the 90-minute score and lands it on your calendar, and anything ambiguous asks you rather than guessing.

Why it works

Two decisions, both awkward.

01

You log at placement, not at settlement

Every tracker that asks you to enter a bet after it finishes gets a record of the ones you wanted to remember. Capturing the slip when you place it is less convenient and it is the only way the total is real.

02

It refuses rather than guesses

Extra time never counts. A whole line on the exact score is a push, not a loss. Handicaps grade differently at bet365 than everywhere else. Anything Slippery cannot be certain of is handed back to you, because a wrong grade is worse than no grade.
