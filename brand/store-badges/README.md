# Store badges

**Nothing in this folder is drawn by hand, and nothing here may be redrawn,
recoloured, cropped, rotated, outlined or set on a background that reduces its
contrast.** Apple and Google both forbid that, and both require a live listing
before the badge may appear at all. Until Slippery has one, the landing page
ships a single line of text instead:

> iOS and Android coming soon. The web app works today.

## Where to drop the official files

| File to save here | Download from |
|---|---|
| `app-store-black.svg` | Apple, Marketing Resources and Identity Guidelines, "Download on the App Store" lockup, SVG, black |
| `google-play.png` | Google Play, Partner Marketing Hub, "Get it on Google Play" badge, English, PNG at 2x or better |

Apple ships SVG. Google ships PNG only and forbids converting it.

## Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space**: on all four sides, at least one quarter of the badge's own
  height. Nothing may enter that box, including the other badge.
- **Apple minimum**: 40px high onscreen, and the badge may never be smaller
  than any other app store badge beside it.
- **Google minimum**: 60px high onscreen when placed alongside Apple's, and
  the two must be optically the same height, which is not the same as the
  same pixel height because the artwork carries different internal padding.
- Both badges must link to the live listing and nowhere else.

## The rule that made this file exist

Neither company permits a modified badge, and "coming soon" written across a
badge is a modification. That is why the coming-soon line on the landing page
is separate text and not artwork, and it stays that way until both listings
are live and the two files above are sitting in this folder.
